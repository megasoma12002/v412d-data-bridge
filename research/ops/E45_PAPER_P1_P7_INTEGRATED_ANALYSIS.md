## 3. Cross-cut synthesis

### 3.1 Intensity vs structure

- **Intensity (α):** P1–P4 converge on **low constant α**. Raising α buys sealed-window cosmetics (P1 sealed prefers higher α) but **destroys held-out score**. That sealed/held-out tension is structural — do not pick α from sealed alone.
- **Structure (where α applies):** P6 is the first screen where a *non-intensity* change beats whole-book A05 on held-out score. Magnitude is small (~**+0.025** score) but directionally consistent with “tax the high-β sleeves, leave the rest”.
- **Timing (when α applies):** P2 rejects crisis gates. Continuous mild overlay > sparse deep cuts on held-out.

### 3.2 Cost and operational realism

- P4: A05 turnover premium vs BASE is tiny (~**+0.04**/yr). Even at **3×** fees, A05 still has score≥0 and positive MDD help.
- Therefore “mild α dies on costs” is **not** supported in this Exact-T+1 early-stack path.

### 3.3 Protection geography (honesty constraint)

- P5: A05’s crisis-year MDD help is almost entirely **2020**. 2022 is a **giveback year** at mild α (−1.92pp MDD). Calm years (2024) show return tax.
- Combined with P7 PAUSE on recent YTD/1y: the book is currently paying **calm/recent giveback** while its historical crisis credit is **narrow**. That is exactly why stitch must stay forbidden and why observe PAUSE is expected, not a bug.
