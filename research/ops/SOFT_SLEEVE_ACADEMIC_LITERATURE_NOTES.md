## Cluster A — Soft / short-horizon info on a slow book (→ Soft-assist)

### A1. Israelov & Katz — *To Trade or Not to Trade?* (AQR / JOIM lineage; classic, still the Soft map)

- **Link:** [AQR summary](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/To-Trade-or-Not-to-Trade-Informed-Trading-With-Short-Term-Signals-for-LongTerm-Investors) · [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1585816)
- **Claim:** Fast-decaying signals often cannot cover stand-alone trading costs. Use them to **filter / time** trades of a slower book (“never trade against the soft view”) instead of standing up a second full-turnover book.
- **Map:** Soft-assist = additive score on top of live `KD_OPT` (assist, don’t replace). Stage A soft OR / champ+ matches this posture.
- **Borrow:** Keep Soft as **name-score assist**; prefer amplitude that informs without forcing a second live book.
- **Do not:** Promote Soft because the soft NAV alone looks good; reopen hard-AND assists already `ASSIST_NO_LIFT`.

### A2. Asness, Ilmanen, Maloney — *Market Timing: Sin a Little* (AQR, 2017; still cited in timing debates)

- **Link:** [AQR](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/Market-Timing-Sin-a-Little)
- **Claim:** Pure valuation timing looks good in-sample but is weak OOS; a **small** dose of momentum / bounded timing is more honest than “all-or-nothing” timing.
- **Map:** Soft amplitude grid (`a025…a15`, sell half-amp) = “sin a little,” not flip live KD.
- **Borrow:** Prefer **small** Soft amplitude changes; Stage A found only modest held lifts (`SELL_a05` 0.101 vs observe 0.085).
- **Do not:** Treat one tip-clean month as license for large Soft α jumps.

## Cluster B — Tactical tilt vs forgone diversification (→ Sleeve-tilt)

### B1. AQR — *Tactical Tilts and Foregone Diversification* (practitioner white paper; core Sleeve hurdle)

- **Link:** [PDF](https://www.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Insights/Alternative-Thinking/Tactical-Tilts-and-Foregone-Diversification.pdf)
- **Claim:** Tactical tilts between sleeves forgo diversification; breakeven skill rises when sleeves are less correlated. Extra turnover must clear costs.
- **Map:** Sleeve-tilt only moves Soft-Frozen **router** weights; KD/TEL unchanged. Month-end tip MDD ALERT on seed = diversification / path risk showing up even when tip CAGR gate PASSes.
- **Borrow:** Keep Sleeve α small; require **tip MDD hygiene** (our Stage A gate) before any promote-shaped claim.
- **Do not:** Auto-increase α after one clean tip month; joint Soft×Sleeve ACCEPT on one ballot.

### B2. de Longis & Ellis — *Tactical Asset Allocation, Risk Premia, and the Business Cycle* (JPM, 2022)

- **Link:** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2022.1.456)
- **Claim:** Regime TAA (leading indicators + risk appetite → recovery/expansion/slowdown/contraction) can add IR ~0.70–0.80 **after costs** in multi-asset examples — as a **bounded overlay**, not a new strategic book.
- **Map:** Sleeve-tilt is our bounded overlay analogue (router MA/RSI), not a fourth live sleeve.
- **Borrow:** Regime-aware **small** tilts + cost honesty; paper IR is not a live badge for us.
- **Do not:** Import their macro indicators into live Soft-Frozen without a new charter.

### B3. Polk, Haghbin, de Longis — *Time-Series Variation in Factor Premia / Business Cycle* (≈2020)

- **Link:** [PDF](http://personal.lse.ac.uk/polk/research/PolkHaghbinDeLongis2020.pdf)
- **Claim:** Factor cyclicality can motivate **dynamic rotation** among factors with IR lift vs static multifactor — after turnover/cost discipline.
- **Map:** Within-FIN Soft scores ≠ factor ETF rotation; still: **dynamic soft weights need cost + OOS gates**.
- **Borrow:** Held-out / sealed windows remain mandatory (we already use them).
- **Do not:** Translate their factor list into Soft-assist hard gates.

## Cluster C — Shrinkage / amplitude / timing complexity (→ Soft amplitude grid)

### C1. Northern Trust — *Dynamic Factor Timing* (industry research; shrinkage toward equal weight)

- **Link:** [PDF](https://www.northerntrust.com/content/dam/northerntrust/investment-management/global/en/documents/research/quantitative/dynamic-factor-timing.pdf)
- **Claim:** In high-dimensional factor timing, **shrinkage** toward equal weight (large λ) stabilizes OOS Sharpe; validation chooses λ.
- **Map:** Soft `a025…a15` and Sleeve `α∈{0.025…0.20}` are our discrete λ grids. Stage A: larger Soft amplitude (`a15`) tip-clean but not always best vs sell-half; Sleeve higher α often fails tip MDD.
- **Borrow:** Treat amplitude as a **hyperparameter with promote-shaped gates**, not a free knob.
- **Do not:** Continuous α search without finite Stage A charter.

### C2. Didisheim, Ke, Kelly, Malamud — *The Virtue of Complexity* (NBER w30217, 2022)

- **Link:** [NBER PDF](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w30217/w30217.pdf)
- **Claim:** Complex return models can help OOS **if** regularized; predictive R² alone understates economic value of timing.
- **Map:** We score with **held MDD↑ − ½|CAGRΔ|**, not R². Complexity without tip MDD hygiene still fails Sleeve seed.
- **Borrow:** Keep composite held score + tip gates; don’t chase in-sample fit.
- **Do not:** Add deep ML Soft books without a new charter and freeze ID.

## Cluster D — Drawdown / MDD hygiene (→ Sleeve tip MDD gate)

### D1. Choi — *Maximum Drawdown, Recovery, and Momentum* (JRFM, 2021)

- **Link:** [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/1911-8074/14/11/542)
- **Claim:** MDD and recovery-based rankings can improve momentum/contrarian selection and risk profiles vs return-only ranks.
- **Map:** Our Stage A **tip MDD clean** gate (YTD+1y MDD not worse than live) is a local, promote-shaped analogue — not their stock-rank rule.
- **Borrow:** Keep tip MDD as **co-gate** with tip CAGR giveback (Sleeve seed fails this).
- **Do not:** Replace live KD with MDD-rank stock picking.

### D2. Hsieh — *Data-Driven Drawdown Control with Restart* (arXiv, 2023)

- **Link:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.02613)
- **Claim:** Hard drawdown caps can morph into stop-loss; a **restart** mechanism avoids permanent freeze after hitting the cap.
- **Map:** Month-end ALERT/PAUSE ≠ cutover license; PAUSE is review, not stop-live Soft-Frozen.
- **Borrow:** ALERT → observe/review; don’t auto-kill dual-paper books on one ALERT.
- **Do not:** Encode hard MDD stop-loss into Soft-Frozen router from this paper alone.

### D3. Drenovak et al. — *Mean–Maximum Drawdown Optimization* (Finance Research Letters, 2022)

- **Link:** [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1544612321003500)
- **Claim:** Explicit Mean–MDD objectives can dominate return-only portfolios in reversing markets.
- **Map:** Supports scoring that **penalizes MDD**, not CAGR alone — matches our held score and tip MDD hygiene.
- **Borrow:** Continue MDD-aware promote shape.
- **Do not:** Swap live objective to Mean–MDD optimizer without charter.
